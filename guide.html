A guaranteed seat at a small, curated table.

Dinner in Paris with 2–4 well-matched strangers, at a restaurant worth showing up for. You pick the cuisine, format, arrondissement and budget. The host picks the company.

  2. Shortlist, not swipesWe rank people interested in that slot; the host invites up to 4.
  3. Guests accept or declineNo rejection visibility — and a “no” can open a new table.
  4. Locked 24h beforeTable number confirmed with the restaurant. Free to join; a no-show fee only if you skip.
